The Problem

Every new chat starts from zero.

Today, a person's intellectual life is scattered across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, notes, drives, and inboxes. Every new session forgets what came before. Context is lost, decisions are re-explained, and progress never accumulates.

Arkana is not another chatbot, and not another model. It is the layer that sits between a person and every AI system they use — so nothing has to be explained twice.

Model-agnostic

AI models are interchangeable compute underneath. Arkana's value is the intellectual history it preserves — not any single model.

Continuous

One environment, not one more window. Memory and context persist across providers, sessions, and years.

Yours

The accumulated intellectual capital — projects, decisions, knowledge — belongs to the person, not the model.
